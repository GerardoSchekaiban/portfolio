Wastewater surveillance of SARS-CoV-2 in Halifax, Nova Scotia

During a research internship in Canada I analyzed four years of wastewater data from four treatment plants. I built a reproducible cleaning pipeline, used clustering to find shared weekly patterns across plants, and tested whether concentration could be predicted. The honest result: the plants moved together, flow did not matter, and the prediction failed, which shows exactly what data is missing.

  • 2,421 → 1,091records after cleaning
  • 94.6%weeks in sync across plants
  • r = 0.02flow vs. concentration
