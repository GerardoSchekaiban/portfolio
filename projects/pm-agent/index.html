PM Agent: automated idea intake and routing

An agentic AI workflow that triages every new product idea the moment it is submitted: it classifies the idea, assigns the right owner, finds similar past ideas and notifies the product manager.

  • n8n
  • LLM (Claude)
  • Vector search
  • Data warehouse
  • Email
  1. S

    Situation

    New product ideas from internal stakeholders were reviewed, categorized and routed by hand by a product management team. There was no practical way to check whether a similar idea had already been submitted, evaluated or rejected elsewhere in the organization, so acknowledging new ideas was slow and inconsistent.

  2. T

    Task

    I was responsible for designing and building an automated system that could take over the entire idea-intake process end to end.

  3. A

    Action

    I designed and built an agentic AI workflow that combines an idea-intake integration, an LLM used as a semantic classifier, a vector-based similarity search over historical submissions, and automated notifications and status updates.

The flow

From a submitted idea to a notified product manager, with no manual step in between.

Result

The system replaced a manual, inconsistent triage step with an automated one that runs the moment an idea is submitted.

5 days → 3 minutes from submission to a correctly assigned owner
  • Eliminated manual idea triage and routing for the product management team.
  • Gave every reviewer visibility into related prior work across the entire organization.
  • Made SLA tracking automatic and consistent instead of calculated by hand for each idea, improving on-time review rates.
  • Replaced keyword-based tagging with semantic understanding, improving the accuracy of idea-to-owner assignment and idea-to-idea matching.
