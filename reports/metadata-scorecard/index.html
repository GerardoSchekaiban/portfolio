      How the score works

      Every table gets a quality score from 0 to 100, calculated in your browser from the table's raw metadata fields. No score is stored anywhere, so every number on the Summary tab can be traced back to a simple rule.

      Coverage vs. quality

      They answer different questions and are deliberately kept apart.

      • Coverage: does documentation exist at all? A table is covered when it has a table description and at least half of its columns are described.
      • Quality: how good is what exists? The average score across the tables in a domain.

      Plotting both gives four situations, each with a different action: high/high is the target, high coverage / low quality needs refinement, low coverage / high quality needs more documentation effort, and low/low is the top priority.
